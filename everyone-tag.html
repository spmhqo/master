Prompt #2
There is one important thing to understand: Facebook does not provide a separate Group Admin setting to switch ON @everyone. The availability of `@everyone` is controlled by Facebook, based on the group's membership size, your role and Facebook's usage limits. Since your group is exclusively for paid members, here is what you should check. ## 1. Check your group size and admin role According to the currently documented Facebook rules: | Group membership | Who can use @everyone | | ------------------- | ------------------------------ | | Up to 100 members | Admins, moderators and members | | 100–200,000 members | Admins and moderators only | | More than 200,000 | Nobody | So, if you are the group admin, you should be eligible even if your group has more than 100 members, provided it has not exceeded 200,000 members. [image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://blog.recurpost.com\&sz=32) RecurPost Blog+1 ## 2. How to use @everyone You don't need to enable anything in Group Settings. 1. Open your private Facebook Group. 2. Open the post where you want to add the comment. 3. Write a comment. 4. Type `@everyone`. 5. Check whether Facebook displays `@everyone` in the suggestion list. 6. Select the suggested `@everyone` option. 7. Complete your comment and publish it. Important: Merely typing `@everyone` as ordinary text does not activate the notification feature. You must select Facebook's suggested tag. ## 3. Check Facebook's usage limits Facebook applies two restrictions: - Individual limit: Each eligible person can use @everyone once per day in a group. - Group limit: There is also a shared seven-day usage limit for the entire group. Facebook does not publicly specify the exact number. If the group has exhausted its weekly allowance, even admins may temporarily lose access to the feature. [image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://blog.recurpost.com\&sz=32) RecurPost Blog+1 ## 4. Can you ensure every paid member receives a notification? Unfortunately, no. Individual members can disable batch-mention notifications in their Facebook settings: Settings & privacy → Settings → Notifications → Tags → Batch mentions Facebook may also withhold notifications based on its notification system. My recommendation for your paid-members-only group I suggest the following: - Keep yourself as the Group Admin. - Use @everyone for important announcements and support-related information. - Avoid using it repeatedly for routine posts. - Keep your support email visible in the first comment. One question: How many members does your private group currently have? That will help me determine which @everyone eligibility rules apply to your group.
