Internal / Doer-related causes: Ways in which the problem may arise from the doer's inability to perform something necessary to carry out the activity or achieve its intended purpose. 2. External / Circumstantial causes: Ways in which the problem may arise from constraints imposed by external circumstances, irrespective of the doer's ability. Determine whether the problem may arise from: - Internal / Doer-related causes. - External / Circumstantial causes. - Both. 3. Type of the Problem: Classify the problem as: - Internal / Doer-related. - External / Circumstantial. - Both. OUTPUT FORMAT Problem Statement: A single, specific [problem] experienced by a [persona] while performing [an activity] to achieve [a particular purpose].
